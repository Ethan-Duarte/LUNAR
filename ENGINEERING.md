# Connected-base screening model

In Advanced → Base, place a habitat, power installation and optional relay/resource/landing facilities. Select each facility to edit its position, role, area or mast height. Use × to remove a facility and Undo to restore it. Recalculate after changes; old results are invalidated.

Solar area, efficiency, derating, panel mode and eclipse geometry determine modeled generation. Load, storage capacity, initial charge, reserve and charge/discharge efficiencies determine the finite-window energy balance. Ending charge matters; a study that survives once is not proof of repeated-cycle sustainability.

Links are sampled straight terrain-clear corridors. The shared electrical bus is idealized: a relay/bus-junction height is not an engineered elevated cable design. Geographic separation is not traverse routing. Pad exclusion distance is a user constraint, not a plume/debris calculation.

Optional RF screening uses free-space link assumptions and supplied gains, frequency, receiver/noise and rate settings. It does not establish operational antenna performance. Earth-centre visibility and station-specific DSN access are distinct. DSN tables cover November–December 2026 and exclude scheduling, weather and capacity.

Use the before/after spatial example to compare a selected 2 m versus 10 m junction. It is a demonstration chosen to expose a connectivity change, not a blind optimization validation. Inspect joint timing, unmet energy, storage requirement and boundary-censored outages before interpreting a result.

Boulders, bearing strength, thermal survival, vehicle dispersion, actual cables, communications operations and launch-plume transport remain unmodeled. Source evidence and numerical checks are in qa and engine/reports.
